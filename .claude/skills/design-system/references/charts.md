# Charts

Use turf-700 for the important series, turf-300 for other series, and turf-100 for fills.
Bars are 40-48px capsules with all corners radius 999; use only horizontal dashed line
grids and no legend for at most two series. Tooltips are custom turf-700 pills. Use
Vietnamese units (`1tr`, `12,4tr`, `1,2 tỷ`) and provide an aria-label or hidden data table.
